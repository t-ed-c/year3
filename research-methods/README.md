# Research Methods in Computer Science

## 📖 Course Overview
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to scientific research methodologies specifically applied to computer science, covering experimental design, data analysis, and academic writing.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Understand different research paradigms in computer science
- Design and conduct empirical studies
- Analyze and interpret research data
- Write technical papers and reports
- Conduct literature reviews effectively
- Apply ethical considerations in research

## 📚 Topics Covered
1. **Research Fundamentals**
   - Research paradigms and methodologies
   - Quantitative vs. qualitative research
   - Research question formulation

2. **Literature Review**
   - Systematic literature review
   - Citation management
   - Critical analysis of papers

3. **Experimental Design**
   - Hypothesis formulation
   - Variable selection
   - Control and treatment groups
   - Statistical power analysis

4. **Data Collection & Analysis**
   - Survey design
   - Interview techniques
   - Statistical analysis methods
   - Data visualization

5. **Academic Writing**
   - Technical paper structure
   - Conference and journal submissions
   - Peer review process

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Reference management: Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote
- Statistical analysis: R, Python (pandas, scipy), SPSS
- Writing: LaTeX, Microsoft Word
- Survey tools: Google Forms, SurveyMonkey
- Data visualization: Tableau, matplotlib, ggplot2

## 📋 Assessment
- Literature review: 25%
- Research proposal: 30%
- Data analysis project: 25%
- Final presentation: 20%
